Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to polascin.net. I respect your privacy and am committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how I look after your personal data when you visit my website and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

2. Information We Collect

This website is primarily informational. We do not require you to create an account.

  • Technical data: Requests may include an IP address, browser details, the requested address, request time and response information. They are used to deliver and secure the site and to diagnose errors. The production log-retention period has not been verified.
  • Local preferences and analytics: Theme and the analytics decision are stored in browser storage. Your selected language is stored in the polascin_lang cookie for up to one year. Google Analytics 4 loads only after you explicitly choose Allow analytics; advertising features remain disabled. You can change or withdraw your choice with Cookie settings in the footer.
  • Necessary session cookie: __Host-POLASCINSESSID is a browser-session cookie used for administrator authentication and form protection. It is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Strict. Authenticated server sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity or 8 hours at the latest.

3. How We Use Your Information

Site operation, security and handling contact messages rely on legitimate interests in running the site and answering enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The newsletter relies on voluntary consent (Article 6(1)(a)), withdrawable at any time by unsubscribing. Email confirmation verifies the subscription request and protects against abuse. Optional analytics requires separate consent.

  • Deliver the website content to you.
  • Ensure the security of the website.
  • Remember your theme preference, selected language and analytics decision.
  • Measure visits and site usage with Google Analytics 4 only after you allow analytics (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw consent at any time using Cookie settings and choosing Decline. The contractual recipients and specific international transfer safeguards still need evidence.

4. Retention

The session cookie lasts for the current browser session. The server ends authenticated sessions after 30 minutes of inactivity or 8 hours at the latest. The polascin_lang language cookie lasts up to one year. Technical access logs are deleted automatically no later than after 90 days, form-protection records after 48 hours, and administrator audit records after 365 days. Contact messages are deleted after 180 days. An unconfirmed newsletter registration is deleted after 72 hours; a confirmed address is kept until unsubscribe. GA4 event and user data retention is set to 14 months. New activity resets the user-data period, so this is not an absolute limit from the first visit. This setting does not affect most standard aggregated reports. Hosting-log retention remains unverified.

5. Recipients and third-party links

Third-party sites linked from this website have their own privacy notices. Websupport provides hosting and the database; according to the operator, it also delivers newsletter emails sent through PHP mail(). Contact messages stay in the portal database and are not supplied to additional contact services; hosting-related processing is not excluded. Following consent, Google receives analytics data through GA4; processing may involve the USA and other third countries. A regional endpoint does not guarantee EEA-only processing. Nefro names SCCs or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, but their applicability to this portal and its contractual recipients still needs evidence. Application access logging is disabled; hosting logs are not thereby excluded.

6. Your rights under the GDPR

Where the GDPR applies and its conditions are met, you may request access, rectification, erasure or restriction of your personal data. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Portability applies only to automated processing based on consent or a contract. You may withdraw consent for consent-based processing at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority; in Slovakia this is the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ). To exercise a right, contact me at the address below.
