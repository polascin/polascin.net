About Me

My work rests at the intersection of medicine, storytelling, and technology. Medicine sharpens my clinical precision; writing lets me explore the human condition through fiction and non-fiction; and technology empowers me to solve complex problems. As a consultant, I bridge these worlds to deliver thoughtful, well-informed solutions.

I approach every challenge with curiosity and rigor — whether I’m treating patients, debugging code, translating nuanced text, or exploring AI.

Nephrology

Nephrology is an essential medical specialty dealing with the kidneys, vital organs responsible for fluid balance, waste filtration, and blood pressure regulation.

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)

Management of progressive loss of kidney function over time due to diabetes, hypertension, or other factors.

Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)

Treatment of sudden, often temporary, loss of kidney function caused by infections, dehydration, or toxins.

Hemodialysis

A procedure where a dialysis machine and a special filter called an artificial kidney, or a dialyzer, are used to clean your blood.

Peritoneal Dialysis

A treatment that uses the lining of your abdomen (belly), called the peritoneum, and a cleaning solution called dialysate to clean your blood.

Transplantation

The best treatment for kidney failure. A healthy kidney is placed into your body to do the work your own kidneys can no longer do.

Diagnostics

Utilizing ultrasound, kidney biopsy, and advanced lab tests to accurately diagnose renal conditions.
